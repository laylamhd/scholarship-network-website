Core Platform Components
1. Scholar & Alumni Profiles
•	Each student would have a personalized profile including:
•	Name, nationality, date of birth
•	Academic institution 
•	Country of study 
•	Degree program 
•	Year of study, joining and graduation date 
•	Skills and interests 
•	Languages spoken 
•	Career aspirations 
•	Volunteer experience 
•	Research interests 
•	Portfolio of achievements 
•	Personal biography 
•	For Alumni, additional information will be requested including years of experience, employment history, certifications, current employer, seniority level, sector, etc.  
•	Students can choose what information is visible publicly within the network.
 
2. Community & Networking Hub
A social networking component where scholars can:
•	Connect with peers 
•	Follow other scholars 
•	Send messages 
•	Join interest groups 
•	Participate in discussions 
•	Create collaborative projects 
•	Form university-specific communities 
•	Form country-specific communities 
•	Form thematic groups (healthcare, education, engineering, humanitarian action, arts, etc.) 
Examples:
•	Palestinian Scholars Network 
•	Rohingya Scholars Community 
•	Women in STEM 
•	Future Humanitarian Leaders 
•	Social Entrepreneurship Group 
 
3. Knowledge & Resource Library
A curated digital repository containing:
•	Academic Resources
•	Study guides 
•	Research tools 
•	Academic writing support 
•	Scholarship renewal guidance 
•	Citation and research tools 
•	Career Resources
•	CV templates 
•	Interview preparation materials 
•	LinkedIn guidance 
•	Career planning tools 
•	Personal Development
•	Leadership courses 
•	Mental health resources 
•	Financial literacy modules 
•	Time management guides 
•	Humanitarian Learning
•	Refugee issues 
•	Sustainable development 
•	Human rights 
•	Social innovation 
•	Community engagement 
 
4. Internship & Career Center
Includes:
•	Opportunities
•	Internships 
•	Graduate trainee programs 
•	Research assistantships 
•	Fellowships 
•	Volunteer opportunities 
•	Job vacancies 
•	Career Services
•	Resume review 
•	Career coaching 
•	Mock interviews 
•	Employer webinars 
 
5. Peer to Peer Mentorship Program
Match scholars with:
•	Alumni Mentors
•	Graduated scholarship recipients.
 
6. Blog & Storytelling Platform
•	Students can publish:
•	Personal stories 
•	Academic journeys 
•	Research insights 
•	Community projects 
•	Cultural experiences 
•	Photography essays 
•	Featured stories can be showcased on TBHF's website and social media channels following approvals from the scholars.
 
7. Multimedia Showcase
•	TBHF can upload:
•	Photos 
•	Videos 
•	Research & Competition posters 
•	Artworks 
•	Presentations 
 
8. Events & Webinars
•	Centralized calendar for:
•	Career workshops 
•	Leadership training 
•	Guest lectures 
•	Networking sessions 
•	University fairs 
•	Scholarship orientation 
•	Features:
•	Registration posters and links for external events and webinars
•	Session recordings 
 
9. Research & Innovation Hub
•	Students can:
•	Publish research summaries 
•	Find collaborators 
•	Share datasets 
•	Submit innovation ideas 
•	View and share research opportunities and challenge competitions 
 
10. Volunteer & Social Impact Portal
Students can:
•	Record volunteer hours 
•	Create community projects 
•	Recruit volunteers 
•	Share project outcomes 
 
11. Alumni Network
After graduation, scholars transition into alumni status.
•	Features:
•	Alumni directory 
•	Career tracking 
•	Mentorship opportunities 
•	Speaking opportunities 
•	Invitations to events and meetups
•	Giving back initiatives 
•	This creates a long-term community rather than a scholarship database.
 
12. Scholar & Alumni Management Module
Administrative features for TBHF.
•	Student Dashboard
•	Number of registered scholarship recipients
•	Number of registered alumni 
•	Demographic breakdown of registered users 
•	Progress reports 
•	Administrator Dashboard
•	Student engagement analytics  
•	Communications management
