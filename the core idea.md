TBHF Global Scholars & Alumni Network 
Concept Note

Vision: A global digital community connecting scholarship recipients supported by The Big Heart Foundation across universities worldwide.
Strategic Objectives
1.	Foster a Global Community
Connect scholarship recipients from diverse countries, cultures, and academic disciplines to encourage collaboration, peer support, and cross-cultural exchange.
2.	Enhance Academic Success
Provide access to handbooks, resources, training opportunities, learning materials, and academic support services that help scholars excel in their studies.
3.	Improve Employability
Equip scholars with professional skills, career guidance, internship opportunities, and employment pathways.
4.	Promote Leadership and Social Impact
Encourage scholars to initiate projects, volunteer, conduct research, and contribute to their communities.
5.	Build a Lifelong Alumni Network
Maintain engagement with graduates and create a growing ecosystem of mentors, professionals, donors, and future leaders.
Core Components
•	Scholar Profiles
•	Community & Networking Hub
•	Knowledge & Resource Library
•	Internship & Career Center
•	Blog & Storytelling Platform
•	Multimedia Showcase
•	Events & Webinars
•	Volunteer & Social Impact Portal
•	Alumni Network
•	Scholarship Management Module
•	Recognition & Gamification
•	Global Map of Scholars
•	Peer to Peer Mentorship
•	Ability for the scholars and alumni to connect directly with TBHF
Long-Term Vision
•	Expand the platform into a global TBHF network of alumni, fellows, interns, researchers, and youth leaders. 
•	AI-Powered Scholar Assistant (Future Phase)
•	Create a sustainable ecosystem that continues generating impact long after scholarship funding ends
•	Include service providers who are able to connect with the scholars and offer additional resources or mentorship through the platform
